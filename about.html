HOW TO PLAY

Pixel Battle is a free drawing game you play in your browser. Everyone gets the same theme, draws it in pixel art before the timer runs out, and then the drawings are scored from 1 to 10. Highest score wins.

THE BASICS

  • Theme: a random theme spins up, like Rocket, Mushroom or Campfire. There are 81 of them.
  • Draw: you get a pixel canvas and a 32-colour palette, with a pen, a big brush, fill, a colour picker, an eraser, mirror drawing, and undo and redo.
  • Vote: when time is up, every drawing is shown one by one and rated from 1 to 10.
  • Win: the highest average score wins. You earn XP every match and level up.

CopycatA picture shows for 5 seconds, then you redraw it from memory. Scored by how closely your pixels match.
ImposterEveryone gets the same word except one secret player, who gets a similar one. Draw, then vote on who the imposter is.
Battle RoyaleUp to 12 artists. The lowest scores are knocked out each round until one artist is left.

HOW SCORING WORKS

The bot judges look at two things: whether the drawing really shows the theme, and how well it is drawn.

SCOREWHAT IT MEANS
1A scribble or a blank canvas
2–4Deformed or messy, but getting there
5–6The right thing with the right colours, but rough
7Clearly looks like the theme
8Really good
9–10Almost perfect, with a great background

A great drawing of the wrong thing still scores low, so stick to the theme.

TIPS FOR BETTER SCORES

  • Draw big. A drawing that fills the canvas reads much better than a tiny one.
  • Start with the main shape, then fill it with the colours people expect (a red apple, a green cactus).
  • Add a dark outline so the shapes stand out.
  • Add a background: sky, ground, stars or a scene. It turns a good drawing into a great one.
  • Finish with small details: a highlight, a shadow, a face.

PLAYING SAFE

There are no accounts and no chat. Names and Charades guesses go through a filter that blocks swearing and other inappropriate words, and a private room's host can remove players.
