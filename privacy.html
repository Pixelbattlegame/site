PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Pixel Battle is a free browser game at pixelbattlegame.com. This page explains what information the game uses and where it goes. The short version: there are no accounts, we don't ask for your email, and your progress stays on your own device.

WHAT IS SAVED ON YOUR DEVICE

The game uses your browser's local storage to remember:

  • the name you type (optional)
  • your level, XP, rank points and settings, such as sound on or off
  • the last themes you drew, so you don't get the same ones again
  • the drawings you save to your Hall of Artworks
  • private rooms whose host removed you, for 15 minutes

This stays in your browser on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

WHAT IS SHARED WHEN YOU PLAY ONLINE

When you play with other people (Find Match, Ranked, Private Room, 2v2, Charades, Picasso), the game sends what's needed to play the match to the other players: your name, your drawings, your votes, your Charades guesses, your level and rank points, the themes you drew recently (so nobody gets a repeat), and a random player ID made for that browser tab. It is passed between players through free public messaging servers run by other organisations:

  • Eclipse Foundation (mqtt.eclipseprojects.io)
  • EMQX (broker.emqx.io)
  • HiveMQ (broker.hivemq.com)

These servers are public, so please don't put personal information in your name. Game messages are only kept while you're playing: they are cleared when you leave, and any left behind are cleared when other players notice you've gone. Quick Play and Practice against bots work entirely on your device. Those operators may keep their own technical logs, such as IP addresses, under their own policies.

WHAT WE DON'T COLLECT

  • No accounts, passwords or email addresses
  • No location, contacts, camera or microphone
  • No chat. The only text other players see is names and Charades guesses, and both go through a filter that blocks inappropriate words.

ADVERTISING

The website may show ads from Google AdSense on the main menu and the results screen. They never appear while you're drawing or voting. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads, which can include ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalised ads in Google's Ad Settings. Find out more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

CHILDREN

Pixel Battle doesn't ask for personal information from anyone. Players under 13 should get a parent's or guardian's permission before playing online, and shouldn't use their real full name as their player name.

OTHER SERVICES

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the pixel fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Like any website, these services receive basic technical information, such as your IP address, when your browser loads the page.

CHANGES AND CONTACT

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. Questions? Open an issue on the game's GitHub page.
